01 — Context

Social casino, built to play together

STASHER is a global developer and publisher of free-to-play mobile games built around social, real-time play. Its flagship titles, STASHER Casino and Billionaire Casino, are social casino games with hundreds of slots, clubs and jackpots, promoted under the line Play Together. The corporate site serves players, candidates, investors and partners from one navigation, with Investors and Careers linking out to their own platforms.

02 — Challenge

Playful, but still a company site

The games are loud, colourful and full of motion; the corporate audience needs clarity and trust. A site that looked like a slot machine would undersell the company to investors and candidates, while a plain corporate template would feel disconnected from the products. The design needed one visual language that could be both, page by page.

The casino energy lives in the composition, not in the typography.

03 — Approach

Dark game worlds, light company pages

Every page follows the same idea: a dark hero that belongs to the game world, night blue on the home page, deep blue for STASHER Casino, emerald for Billionaire Casino, and a light page underneath for the company content. One red diagonal line cuts between the two. Chips, balls and emoji float across that cut as separate layers, so the casino energy sits in the composition while the copy stays plain.

05 — Details

Fewer objects, plain copy

Each section uses only a few floating objects, placed to frame a headline or a phone rather than cover it. The game pages share one template: a hero with three key numbers and store buttons, Hit the Jackpot with phone screens, a six-item feature grid and a closing Play for free band. Roboto throughout keeps the copy plain next to the louder art.

06 — Outcome

One language for players and investors

The site feels unmistakably like STASHER without behaving like a game. Players reach the games and support in one click, while candidates and investors get calm, readable pages. The game page template already carries two titles, and a new one only needs its own art and palette. WMC agency and KCA University built the site from my designs.
