Questions

How long does a project take?
It follows your plan. Tell me the dates you have agreed with the client and I will say plainly whether they fit. I reply to every brief within two working days.
How do you price agency work?
There is no price list. I quote after the brief, for the scope we agree on, so you can build it into your own estimate.
Can you work with our developers, or the client's?
Yes, either. I join reviews, hand over components and check the build against the design, as I did for WMC on Urban Sanaagaz.
Which tools do you use?
Figma, in your files and your libraries. React, Next.js and GSAP for prototypes in code. I build in WordPress, Next.js or HTML myself, and in Framer or Webflow with partner developers I work with. Cursor to work faster.
Do you work remotely?
Yes. I'm based in Nairobi, Kenya, on Central European Time, and work with teams remotely: calls, shared Figma files and your channels.
