How I work

  1. 01

    Listen

    A short call and a written brief: who the product is for, what done looks like, what must not break.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    The key flows go into a clickable prototype early, often in code, so decisions rest on something you can use.

  3. 03

    Build together

    Structured revisions with mockups and real-world tests.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Documentation, components and a team that can carry the product on without me.

Questions

How long does a project take?
It depends on the scope. After the brief I propose stages and dates, and we agree on them before the work starts. I reply to every brief within two working days.
How do you price a project?
There is no price list. I quote after the brief, for the scope we agree on. A rough budget range in the form helps me suggest the right scope.
Can you work with our in-house developers?
Yes, that is how I work best. I join reviews, hand over components and check the build in the browser. I prototype and build in React, Next.js or HTML myself, so the handover speaks your team's language.
Which tools do you use?
Figma for the design and the design system. React, Next.js and GSAP for prototypes in code. Cursor to work faster.
Do you work remotely?
Yes. I'm based in Nairobi, Kenya, on Central European Time, and work with teams remotely: calls, shared Figma files and your channels.
