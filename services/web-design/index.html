How I work

  1. 01

    Listen

    A short call and a written brief: who visits, what they should do next, what must not break.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    The key pages go into a clickable prototype early, motion included, so we judge the real thing, not a picture of it.

  3. 03

    Build together

    I work with the developers from day one: reviews, handover and the details checked in the browser.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    A site your team can edit, with the components and notes on how they fit together.

Questions

How long does a website take?
It depends on the number of pages and how much motion they carry. After the brief I propose stages and dates, and we agree on them before the work starts. I reply to every brief within two working days.
How do you price a website?
There is no price list. I quote after the brief, for the scope we agree on. A rough budget range in the form helps me suggest the right scope.
Can you work with our developers?
Yes. I design with the build in mind, hand over components and review the site in the browser. On Urban Sanaagaz I audited the build against the design before launch.
Which tools do you use?
Figma to design; React, Next.js and GSAP for prototypes in code. I build in WordPress, Next.js or HTML myself; Framer and Webflow sites are built by partner developers I work with. Cursor to work faster.
Do you work remotely?
Yes. I'm based in Nairobi, Kenya, on Central European Time, and work with teams remotely: calls, shared Figma files and your channels.
